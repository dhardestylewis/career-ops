# User Custom Rules -- career-ops

## Missing-Bridge Rule

- If a reply, intro, or follow-up depends on a person, bridge, event, or other Daniel-only fact that is not present in connected sources or repo evidence, stop and ask Daniel for the missing detail.
- Do not guess, infer, or keep the thread moving with a placeholder when the exact name or fact is required.
- This applies especially when a recipient refers to "the person from Germany," "the one who relocated," or any similarly vague bridge that only the human may know.

## Thank-You Discipline Rule

- After any calendar-visible call or meeting, the default is to send one brief thank-you follow-up within 24 hours in the same channel where the conversation is already happening.
- When auditing outreach discipline, check Gmail, Slack, and LinkedIn DM for that follow-up and treat missing evidence as "not sent."
- If live access to one of those channels is unavailable, say the audit is partial rather than inferring discipline from templates, memory, or sparse examples.
- Prefer concise gratitude tied to one specific detail from the conversation, not a generic thank-you.
