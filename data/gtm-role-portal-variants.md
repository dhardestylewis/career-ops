# GTM Role Portal Variants

Date: 2026-07-07

Use this note when a new agent needs portal-ready versions of the Homecastr role families. The site page stays canonical; these are shorter, more portable variants for job boards, community boards, and related portals.

## Fill Rules

- Keep the portal title honest and concise.
- Keep the body to two short paragraphs or fewer.
- Lead with what the role owns, not the brand story.
- Use the same family as the careers page unless a portal forces a shorter title.
- If the portal requires a generic label, keep the body specific.

## Variants

| Canonical role | Portal title | Portal body | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Automation Engineer / Agentic Workflow Engineer | AI Automation Engineer | Build internal automations and agentic workflows that remove manual work and improve reliability across the company. Best fit for someone who can ship dependable systems and light-touch AI tooling. | General job boards, technical communities |
| Growth Operations Engineer | Growth Ops Engineer | Turn lead routing, targeting, and growth plumbing into a repeatable operating loop. Best fit for an operator who likes clean systems, fast iteration, and clear handoffs. | Startup boards, operator communities |
| Performance Marketing Ops Analyst | Marketing Ops Analyst | Keep paid channels honest with pacing, anomaly detection, and QA. Best fit for someone who spots issues early and is comfortable with detail. | Growth and performance-marketing boards |
| Client Reporting / RevOps Automation Specialist | RevOps Automation Specialist | Automate onboarding, reporting, and customer updates so the team can scale without adding friction. Best fit for someone who can make the boring parts feel effortless. | RevOps and customer-ops portals |
| Finance & Vendor Operations Analyst | Finance Ops Analyst | Track subscriptions, vendors, reconciliation, and leakage before it compounds. Best fit for someone who is comfortable with detail and accountability. | Finance-ops, ops, and analyst boards |
| Sales Proposal Automation / GTM Systems Contractor | GTM Systems Contractor | Build proposal, follow-up, and sales support systems that help GTM move faster. Best fit for a contractor who can move quickly and keep copy tight. | Contractor portals, GTM ops boards |

## Notes

- If a portal only allows one title, use the portal title column.
- If a portal allows a subtitle, use the canonical role in the subtitle and the portal title in the main title.
- If the portal is highly technical, lean harder on automation, workflow, and systems language.
- If the portal is community-oriented, lean harder on collaboration and operating leverage language.
