# GTM Outreach Review

Date: 2026-07-13

## Bottom Line

GTM outreach is clearer than the career lane, but it is still pre-call heavy. Email is the strongest channel. The bottleneck is turning replies into booked and then held conversations.

## Raw Ledger Totals

- Ledger rows: 146
- Send rows: 131
- Reply rows: 7
- Pending rows: 3
- Blocked rows: 5
- Important gap: the ledger does not store held discovery calls as first-class outcomes, so the outcome-backfill remains the stage-level source of truth.

## What Works

- One hook, one proof point, one ask.
- Email-led compare-notes outreach.
- Warm reconnects and direct replies.
- Routing notes that make it easy to point you to the right owner.
- Short messages that can be answered in one glance.

## What Is Missing

- Confirmed held discovery calls.
- Explicit stage logging for booked versus held conversations.
- Clear separation between customer / partner GTM and investor / fundraising outreach.
- Better capture of warm handoffs and follow-on steps.

## What To Change

1. Track booked discovery separately from held discovery.
2. Track warm handoffs separately from ordinary replies.
3. Keep investor / fundraising in the VC handoff lane.
4. Keep the CTA tiny and specific.
5. Use LinkedIn mainly for routing checks, not as the primary conversion channel.

## Practical Fix

The fastest win is not more sends. It is tighter follow-through on the replies already landing, plus better stage capture once the thread turns into a call.
