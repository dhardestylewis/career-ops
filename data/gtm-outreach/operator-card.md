# GTM Outreach Operator Card

Date: 2026-07-13

Use this first if you are resuming GTM outreach.

## Current Read

- Email is the dominant channel.
- LinkedIn mostly serves routing checks and connect-note verification.
- The main leak is reply -> booked discovery -> held discovery.
- If a target is an investor, allocator, or fundraising advisor, route it to `docs/homecastr-vc-agent-handoff.md` and keep it out of the GTM lane.
- Booked discovery counts as a real conversion only when source-linked.
- Held discovery is the real bottom-of-funnel event.

## Next 100 Goal

- Keep GTM separate from job search and from VC / investor outreach.
- Turn replies into booked calls, then into held calls, then into explicit next steps.
- Keep every ask to one hook, one proof point, and one small CTA.

## Operating Rule

- Draft first.
- Do not send until the dossier is complete and source-backed.
- If the next draft only paraphrases the last touch, stop and wait for a real reply or a new source-backed angle.
- If the recipient moved roles, rewrite as a reconnect or current-role note.
- If a reply needs judgment, stop and notify the user.

## What To Preserve

- Short sentences.
- One hook.
- One proof point.
- One ask.
- Clear routing language.

## What Not To Do

- Do not write a job-application style note unless the context is actually hiring.
- Do not stack several asks into one message.
- Do not lead with your full backstory.
- Do not sound like a mass outreach sequence.
- Do not use vague openers like "just checking in".
- Do not send if the conflict check is unclear for a revenue, partnership, or customer pitch.
