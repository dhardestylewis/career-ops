# GTM Outreach Scripts

Use these as first drafts, then swap in the recipient-specific details.

## 1) Inbound Lead Acknowledgement

**Use when:** someone reached out, filled out a form, replied to a prompt, or otherwise showed clear intent.

**Subject:** `Re: [topic]`

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out about [topic]. I saw [specific trigger], and I think there may be a useful fit.

I have been working on [proof point]. If helpful, I can share a bit more or point you to the best next step.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 2) Cold Outbound Lead Note

**Use when:** you are reaching out to a prospect, customer, partner, or operator with no warm intro.

```text
Hi [Name] - I saw your work on [specific thing]. I have been building [proof point], and I thought it might be worth connecting if you are working on [problem].

Would a short chat be useful?
```

## 3) Warm Intro or Reconnect

**Use when:** there is a referral, a prior thread, or a clear shared history.

```text
Hi [Name],

I wanted to pick back up on our earlier context around [bridge]. Since then I have been working on [proof point], and I thought it could be worth a quick reconnect if useful.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 4) Partner or Ecosystem Note

**Use when:** the recipient is a founder, operator, advisor, community lead, or routing contact.

```text
Hi [Name] - your work on [specific thing] stood out. I think there may be a useful bridge around [shared topic], and I wanted to ask whether you are the right person to speak with.
```

## 5) Discovery Follow-Up

**Use when:** the first note went out and there has not been a reply yet.

```text
Hi [Name] - I wanted to keep this simple. The reason I reached out still feels relevant because [new signal or proof point].

If it is helpful, I am happy to keep the thread short or route to the right person.
```

## 6) Meeting Confirmation

**Use when:** the thread has moved into a live conversation or scheduled call.

```text
Hi [Name] - looking forward to speaking on [date]. I appreciated your work on [specific thing] and have been digging into [relevant detail] so we can keep the conversation focused.
```

## Fill Rules

- Replace only the bracketed text.
- If you need a second proof point, shorten the message instead.
- If the message becomes a pitch deck, it is too long.
- If the contact dossier is incomplete, do not send yet. Mark the row `research` and gather the missing source material first.
- If the recipient moved roles, rewrite the opener around the current role rather than pretending the old context still applies.

## Banned / Low-Performing Phrasing

- `just checking in`
- `touching base`
- `circling back`
- `I am passionate about`
- `wanted to reach out and see if there are opportunities`

## Tone Rules

- Short sentences.
- One hook.
- One proof point.
- One ask.
- No corporate filler.
- No mass-blast language.
- No multi-paragraph biography.
