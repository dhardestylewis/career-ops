# GTM Outreach Translator

Use this when adapting the job-search outreach system for GTM lead, customer, or partner work.

## Translation Map

| Job-search habit | GTM equivalent |
|---|---|
| application | lead, opportunity, inquiry |
| applicant tracking | CRM, lead queue, pipeline |
| recruiter | partner, operator, routing contact, ops lead |
| hiring manager | decision maker, owner, champion |
| interview | discovery call |
| resume or CV | proof pack, one-pager, short background |
| portfolio | case study, product proof, demo, customer story |
| score | lead score, fit score |
| follow-up | nudge, sequence, next touch |
| company fit | buyer fit, use-case fit, partner fit |
| company research | account research, account plan |
| referral | warm intro, internal champion |
| career services | community router, connector, advisor |
| interview prep | discovery prep |
| application email | outbound note, intro email |

## What Carries Over

- One hook.
- One proof point.
- One ask.
- Source-backed wording only.
- Short sentences.

## What Changes

- Replace job title language with lead or account language.
- Replace resume talk with product or customer proof.
- Replace interview language with discovery language.
- Replace recruiter language with routing, partner, or owner language.
- Replace application cadence with lead follow-up cadence.

## Wording Rule

If a sentence sounds like it belongs in a job application, rewrite it until it sounds like a real GTM conversation.
